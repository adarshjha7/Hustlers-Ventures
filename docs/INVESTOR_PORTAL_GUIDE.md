# Hustlers Ventures — Investor Portal Guide

Welcome to the Hustlers Ventures Investor Portal. This guide walks you through everything available on the platform so you can track your investments, understand your payouts, and stay informed.

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Open your browser and go to `https://hustlersventures.co/login`
2. Enter your registered email address and click **Send OTP**
3. Check your email for a one-time password (OTP) — it arrives within a minute
4. Enter the OTP and click **Verify**
5. You will be taken to your investor dashboard

> If you have not received your login credentials, contact your relationship manager.

### First Login

On your first login you may be prompted to set a new password. Choose a secure password you will remember — you will use it for all future logins.

### Forgot Password

1. On the login page, click **Forgot Password**
2. Enter your registered email and click **Send Reset Link**
3. Check your email for a reset link (valid for 1 hour)
4. Click the link and set your new password

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your home screen. It shows a snapshot of everything happening with your portfolio.

### Capital Deployed

The large number at the top shows your **total capital deployed** — the sum of all amounts you have invested across all assets. This is your principal, not including returns.

### Earned Chips

Below the main number you will see small chips showing:
- **+₹X earned** — total returns paid out to you so far (lifetime)
- **In reserve** — funds held back as an emergency reserve from your payouts
- **Last year IRR** — your annualised return rate over the past 12 months

### Last Payout

A mini card on the right shows the date and amount of your most recent payout.

### Portfolio PDF

Click **Download Portfolio PDF** to get a one-page summary of your portfolio. Useful for your own records or sharing with a chartered accountant.

## Your Assets

### Asset Breakdown

Each investment you hold appears as a card. Each card shows:
- **Asset name** — the pool or fund you are invested in
- **Total invested** — your capital in that specific asset
- **Total payouts** — cumulative returns received from this asset
- **IRR** — your annualised return rate for this asset
- **Capital returned** — a progress bar showing what percentage of your original capital has been returned through payouts

You can sort the asset cards by invested amount, payouts, or IRR using the sort buttons above.

## Payouts

### Payout Trajectory Chart

The chart below your asset cards shows your payout history over time. You can:
- Switch between **Net** (after TDS and deductions) and **Gross** (before deductions) amounts
- Filter by time range: **6M**, **1Y**, **3Y**, or **All**
- Hover over any bar to see the breakdown per asset for that period

### Historical Performance

Switch to the **Historical Performance** tab to see your year-by-year returns compared to benchmark returns (Fixed Deposit and Nifty 50). Green bars indicate years where your returns exceeded 22%.

### Understanding Deductions

Each payout has two components:
- **Gross amount** — the full return before deductions
- **Emergency fund deduction** — a small percentage held back as a reserve (returned to you at exit)
- **TDS** — tax deducted at source (as required by law)
- **Net payable** — the amount actually credited to your bank account

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When are payouts credited?**
Payouts are processed quarterly. You will receive an email notification when your payout has been initiated. Funds are credited to your registered bank account within 2–3 business days of processing.

**What is the emergency fund deduction?**
A small percentage (typically 5%) is held back from each payout as a reserve. This is returned to you when you exit the investment. It protects the pool against unforeseen operational costs.

**What is TDS?**
TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is deducted as required under Indian tax law. You will receive a TDS certificate at the end of the financial year which you can use to claim credit when filing your income tax return.

**How do I update my bank account details?**
Contact your relationship manager or use the Help button to raise a request. Bank account changes require verification before they take effect.

**Can I add more capital to an existing investment?**
Yes — contact your relationship manager to initiate a top-up. The additional capital is linked to your existing investment record and starts earning returns from the next declaration cycle.

**How do I exit an investment?**
Exit terms depend on the specific pool agreement. Contact your relationship manager to initiate an exit request. The process typically takes 30–60 days depending on liquidity.
